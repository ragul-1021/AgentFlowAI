EU AI Act: what shipping teams must document

2,040 WORDS 7 SOURCES 22 CLAIMS REVISION 3 OF 3 22 NOV 14:02
Three automatic revisions are spent and one claim is still unsupported. The gate is holding for a person. Open the decision

1Why documentation is now a shipping gate

Since 2 August 2026 the documentation duties for high-risk systems are enforceable, and they attach to the provider rather than to the deployment. That shift matters for engineering teams: the artefact is no longer a compliance appendix written after launch, it is a build output that has to exist before the system is placed on the market.S1

A useful test is whether the pack would let a competent engineer who has never seen the system reproduce its risk assessment. If it would not, the pack is marketing.

2What counts as a high-risk system

Classification is not a judgement about how capable a model is. Annex III lists the contexts of use that trigger it — employment, education, essential services, migration, and several others — and a system can be high-risk while being technically simple.S3

Two exemptions are worth knowing: a system that performs a narrow procedural task, and one that only detects patterns without replacing human assessment. Both are narrow, and the burden of arguing them sits with the provider.S2

3The technical documentation pack

Annex IV is the closest thing to a specification. It asks for nine areas, from a general description of the system through risk management, standards applied, and the evaluation of accuracy and robustness.S7

Most of these are documents a competent team already produces somewhere. The work is not writing new prose; it is gathering what exists and making it traceable to a version of the system.

4Logging and traceability

Article 19 requires providers to keep automatically generated logs for a period appropriate to the intended purpose, and sets a floor of six months. The logs have to allow the identification of events relevant to traceability and monitoring.S6

Providers must retain those logs for a minimum of ten years, and make them available to authorities on request.?

5Post-market monitoring

A post-market monitoring plan is a separate artefact from the technical file, and it has to be proportionate to the risk. It is where you say what you will measure after release, how often, and who reads the result.S5

The common failure is a plan that measures model quality in aggregate but cannot answer a question about a single affected person.

6A checklist you can hand to an engineer

Four questions cover most of the gap: which version of the system is this pack about, which Annex IV item does this document satisfy, who signs the risk assessment, and what would trigger a re-assessment.

None of this is exotic. It is the ordinary discipline of shipping software, written down far enough in advance that someone else can audit it.
